How to use Google Drive Links

Follow these simple steps so both you and your partner can view photos stored in Google Drive:

  1. 1 Upload your photo to Google Drive.
  2. 2 Right-click the photo and choose Share ➔ Share.
  3. 3 Under General Access, change option to "Anyone with the link can view".
  4. 4 Copy link and paste it into the Add Memory form on this app!
